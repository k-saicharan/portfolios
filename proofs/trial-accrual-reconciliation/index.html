Two-System Clinical Trial Accrual Reconciliation

When hospital clinical databases meet national research portfolios, participant counts diverge. A deterministic reconciliation model isolates orphan records, dedupes participant keys, and flags recruitment velocity bottlenecks before statutory returns.

The operational gap

  • Disparate research systems drift out of sync. Hospital trusts manage local clinical trial enrollment in platforms such as EDGE and local Electronic Patient Records (EPR), while statutory reporting flows into central registries like the NIHR Open Data Platform.
  • Silent data anomalies distort portfolio metrics. Multi-arm trials create duplicate consent timestamps; study protocol amendments change recruitment targets mid-cycle; and manual participant ID entry leads to unmapped study codes.
  • Cumulative totals mask failing trials. Reporting total participants recruited to date hides whether recruitment velocity has flatlined in the final three months before trial close-out.

The reconciliation primitive

Two-List Anti-Join & Duplicate Quarantine (FP-DATA-01)

Before aggregating research accruals or publishing clinical trial scorecards, validate records through a four-stage deterministic gate:

  1. Deduplication: Partition the local patient intake on pseudonymised patient key and study code, retaining strictly the earliest confirmed consent timestamp.
  2. Orphan Isolation: Execute an anti-join against the national portfolio master registry to stage unmatched records into a quarantine exception table with specific error codes (e.g. ERR_UNMAPPED_STUDY, ERR_INVALID_SITE).
  3. Velocity Measurement (FP-BI-02): Compute dynamic recruitment velocity: remaining target patients divided by active run-rate times months remaining. Ratios exceeding 1.25 immediately trigger operational intervention flags.
  4. Clean Model Output: Pass strictly verified, reconciled accruals to the Power BI reporting data model.

The SQL translation

-- 1. Deduplicate local accruals on unique participant + study key
WITH deduped_accruals AS (
  SELECT 
    pseudo_id,
    study_id,
    site_code,
    consent_date,
    ROW_NUMBER() OVER (
      PARTITION BY pseudo_id, study_id 
      ORDER BY consent_date ASC
    ) AS record_rank
  FROM local_edge_accruals
),

-- 2. Anti-join against central trial registry to quarantine orphans
orphaned_exceptions AS (
  SELECT 
    d.*,
    'ERR_UNMAPPED_STUDY' AS exception_reason
  FROM deduped_accruals d
  LEFT JOIN nihr_portfolio_master p 
    ON d.study_id = p.study_id
  WHERE d.record_rank = 1 
    AND p.study_id IS NULL
)
SELECT * FROM orphaned_exceptions;

What this changes for a research informatics team

  • Eliminates end-of-quarter manual data scrubbing across spreadsheets.
  • Provides clinical research coordinators with a daily exception queue showing exactly which participant records need protocol mapping.
  • Ensures portfolio dashboards present trustworthy, audit-ready numbers that protect NHS trust research infrastructure funding.
