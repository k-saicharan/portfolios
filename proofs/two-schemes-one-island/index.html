Two deposit schemes, one island

Today, a drinks pack on the island of Ireland either carries a deposit or doesn't. From 1 October 2027, Northern Ireland packs carry their own deposit too. The risk changes from "deposit vs none" to "the wrong deposit".

Where things stand

Deposit15c or 25c, depending on container size20p

On-pack markingRe-turn logo, plus a national (RoI-only) barcode for scheme packsScheme label required on in-scope packs

Today NI packs carry different barcodes from RoI scheme packs, and no Re-turn logo. So a barcode scan can already tell them apart. Whether a given warehouse system blocks the wrong one at despatch is site-specific and unverified here.

What changes for all-island distribution

  1. Three pack versions where there were two. RoI-scheme, NI-scheme and (for some routes) non-scheme export packs of the same drink. Each needs its own barcode, master-data record and stock location.
  2. Mistakes cost money on both sides. An RoI pack sold in NI carries a deposit that NI customers can't reclaim, and once the UK scheme is live the reverse is also true. Today only one direction carries a deposit.
  3. Master data is the control point, not the floor. If the GTINs differ and the system checks them at pick and despatch, the floor is already safe. Risk moves to where barcodes are created, copied or relabelled: new product set-up, promotional packs, cross-docks and wholesale breaks.
  4. Reporting doubles. Two scheme operators means two sets of packs-placed-on-market returns, each tied to the right market.

The one-aisle check

One evening's test answers whether a floor check adds anything:

  1. Take one RoI-scheme pack and one NI pack of the same drink.
  2. Photograph both labels and both barcodes.
  3. Scan both. Do they resolve to different products in the warehouse system? Does despatch to the wrong market get blocked?

If it blocks: the floor is solved, and the work is the master-data plan for 2027. If the barcodes match: a visual check at the bay is worth building.

Questions to put to a supply chain team

  • Will NI-scheme packs get new GTINs, or will current NI codes carry the UK label?
  • Where in the network are pallets broken and re-picked across the border?
  • Who owns pack-market master data, and is there a check when a promo pack is set up?
