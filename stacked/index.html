Why it exists

A shift log you can keep while picking.

Stacked is the log I actually use: one tap per pallet, on the phone that is already in the other hand.

How it works
  1. Tap the item count as you finish each pallet. One hand, no typing, no menus mid-shift.
  2. The app timestamps and logs it instantly, and auto-detects Morning / Evening / Night from the clock, including midnight crossings.
  3. Pace and daily target stay two separate numbers. They are not collapsed into one figure called "speed."
  4. Drop a quick timestamped note for anything that slowed you down (dock delay, forklift queue, system down) so the log explains itself later.
  5. Set your rotation once and the app forecasts your upcoming shift pattern automatically.
Two numbers

Pace is how fast the pallets actually moved. Daily target is what the shift is supposed to clear after breaks. They use different inputs on purpose, so the app cannot quietly turn them into one "speed" number.

Where the data sits
  • No accounts Open the app and use it. No email, username, or login.
  • On the phone Logs, stats, and notes stay on the device.
  • Opt-in sync Sync is off until you turn it on.
  • If it syncs Only pallet count, item count, and duration. Notes and clock times stay local.
