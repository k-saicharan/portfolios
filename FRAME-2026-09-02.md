## What the jobs URL is for

One sentence: give a stranger who already skimmed the CV one specific, checkable reason to reply.

- After 20 seconds they must believe: this person was somewhere real, saw something the systems above them could not see, and something outside his notes is now different.
- After 3 minutes they must believe: that came from a way of deciding, and I can predict Monday in my building.

"Here is how my brain works" cannot produce the 20-second belief. The 3-minute belief is the mind, reached only through a fork with a cost.

CANON's "lead with the mind" was correct against a credentials ledger. It was misread as "lead with an essay about thinking." Resolution: lead with the mind as a claim about a specific real situation. Not "I think things through." Something closer to: the number the system trusted was the number that got confirmed rather than the number that was true, and here is what changed when I noticed.

## Inclusion / exclusion

**Unit:** a situation he was standing in, what he noticed that the system did not, and the thing outside his head that is now different. From his own line: "how my mind works when it is thrown into a situation and left there for a while."

**In:** Was he in the situation, and did something outside his notes change because he was? A proposal that got answered counts. A case a company closed counts. A handwritten list replaced by a phone counts. Deployed-at-employer is not required.

**Out:** If the only evidence it exists is his writing about it, it is a seed. It stays in the vault. No card, no section, no "how I think" heading. The moment a vault note has a heading on the jobs surface, `unseen/` is back.
