STACKED · ANDROID 2026 · 06–08
BUILT · PILOT (SINGLE USER)

Stacked: one-handed shift logging with a worker threat model

Born on the floor: paper shift logs lose where a shift actually goes, and any tool that logs output can become a surveillance feed. Stacked is a Flutter Android app (package com.saicharan.stacked) for warehouse pickers: log pallets one-handed mid-shift, see pace and targets with explicit formulas, never one ambiguous number called "speed."

The design decision that matters for hiring: when a shift syncs, wall-clock start/end timestamps are stripped; only duration and aggregate counts leave the device. Free-text notes never leave the phone. Consent is explicit opt-in. Manager-side access is a summary view, not a replay of when you stood where. Built by the person who would be on the wrong end of getting that wrong.

Status: I use it on shift. Pilot target ~25–30 workers is not live yet. Source stays private while real exports sit in backups; the app and its privacy model are written up on their own page.

Two formulas, deliberately different denominators. They may never be merged into one vague "speed."

SAP EWM · VOICE MIDDLEWARE 2026 · 05
PROTOTYPE · NOT DEPLOYED

A correction layer between headset and warehouse system

Voice-directed picking fails on accented speech in a high-noise bay. The prototype is a per-worker word map between headset middleware and SAP EWM. SAP is not touched. Industry pattern that survives is the same shape: thin intelligence over load-bearing legacy, not rip-and-replace. I proposed it to continuous improvement; some cards engaged, some did not. That non-adoption is part of the record, not a failure to hide.

Escalation as architecture

A multi-week consumer order that crossed six operational systems. Each layer examined from the inside: what it could see, what it structurally could not, and where handoffs dropped. Locally green systems, aggregate failure, nobody's KPI breached. Same shape as warehouse "everything is green" friction, different building. VLM · TOTE-COUNTER 2026 · 02
BUILT · OPEN SOURCE

Counting tote stacks with a vision model

A recurring miscount, turned into a working tool. Vision-language model with a chain-of-thought prompt that counts edges in sequence rather than guessing the whole frame. FastAPI backend, phone in the aisle. Accurate to within one unit on the test set. Cheap proof that measurement that used to need a clipboard can start from a phone.
